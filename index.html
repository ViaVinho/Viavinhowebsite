Who we are

Not a company.
A point of view
about wine.

Via Vinho was built around a simple conviction: the best way to understand Portuguese wine is to stand in the vineyard, meet the people who make it, and sit down to a long lunch with a glass in your hand.

Based in Lisbon, we work exclusively with independent estates — family-run wineries with something genuine to say. No scripted tastings. No bus groups. No obvious itineraries.

"We go to places that don't advertise. That's the point."
