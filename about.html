Who we are

Not a company.
A point of view
about wine.

Via Vinho was built around a simple conviction: the best way to understand Portuguese wine is to stand in the vineyard, meet the people who make it, and sit down to a long lunch with a glass in your hand.

Based in Lisbon. Operating since 2012.

The story

We go to places
that don't advertise.

Via Vinho started because of a frustration. The wine tours that existed were designed for people who needed to be entertained. We wanted something for people who were genuinely curious, who wanted to understand, not just taste.

We work exclusively with independent estates, family-run wineries with something genuine to say and no obligation to perform for tour groups. Some of them have never hosted a commercial tour before us. That trust has been earned over more than a decade of showing up the right way.

"No scripted tastings. No bus groups. No obvious itineraries."

Our guides are not characters. They are people who genuinely love this work, who know Portuguese wine at a level most sommeliers in London or New York would envy, and who understand that the best days are the ones that feel completely unplanned, even when every detail has been thought through in advance.

Via Vinho Reserve came later, built for the guests who kept asking: could we do this privately? Could we stay the night in Alentejo? Could we meet the winemaker? The answer was always yes. We just had to make it official.

What we believe

The principles behind
every experience.

01

Independence, always

We work with family-run estates only. Never with cooperatives, large commercial producers, or anyone who pays for placement. The recommendation is always honest.

02

Small, by design

Eight guests maximum, without exception. The number is not a selling point. It is the thing that makes every other part of the experience possible.

03

The table matters

Lunch is not a logistics problem to be solved. It is as important as the tasting. We have spent years finding the right tables and earning the right reservations.

04

No performance

Our guides don't present. They converse. There is no script, no theatrical vocabulary, no attempt to impress. Just genuine expertise offered honestly.

05

Access over aesthetics

We go to places because they make interesting wine, not because they photograph well. Some of the best estates we visit are completely anonymous from the road.

06

Unhurried, always

We do not try to fit more in. A long lunch is not a delay. A conversation in the cellar is not an interruption. The pace is part of the experience.
