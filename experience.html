Palmela · Azeitão · Full Day

The Palmela Salon.

A prestige tasting, a wine-meets-art immersion with Filipe Palhoça, and a long lunch with regional cuisine. Layered, sensory, unhurried.

Palmela sits above the Setúbal Peninsula like a watchtower over one of Portugal's most underrated wine regions. The castle, the old town, the views south to the Arrábida mountains — it all feels like a secret that the rest of the country forgot to share.

This day begins with a prestige tasting at Adega de Palmela — a cooperative with a cellar that will change your understanding of Moscatel de Setúbal. Then we drive to Azeitão to meet Filipe Palhoça, whose tasting room blurs the line between wine and art. His work is extraordinary. So is his Castelão.

Lunch is at a table we've been earning for years. Regional cuisine, estate wines, and the kind of time that only exists when nobody is watching the clock.

The day.

Morning · Palmela

A prestige tasting at Adega de Palmela, guided by the estate's head winemaker. Moscatel de Setúbal across three decades.

Late Morning · Azeitão

Filipe Palhoça's tasting room. Wine as creative practice. A private session with one of the region's most singular producers.

Afternoon · Lunch

A long table at a restaurant we've known for years. Regional dishes, estate wines, and no reason to leave early.

Late Afternoon · Return

Back to Lisbon via the Arrábida coast road. If the light is right, we stop.
