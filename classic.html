What is Classic

Small groups.
Real access.

Via Vinho Classic is for those who want to discover Portuguese wine properly, with someone who genuinely knows it. Small groups, independent estates, and a pace that allows the day to breathe.

We work exclusively with family-run wineries that have something real to say. No scripted presentations. No bus groups. No obvious itineraries.
